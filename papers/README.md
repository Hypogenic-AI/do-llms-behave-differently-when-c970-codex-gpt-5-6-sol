# Downloaded papers

All 17 PDFs were opened successfully with `pypdf`. The 11 papers named in the research specification were deep-read in full using the chunk manifests in `papers/pages/`; six additional papers were selected because they directly address surface confounds, behavioral consequences, interlocutor identity, or measurement validity.

| # | Paper | Authors | Year | Local file | Why it matters |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|---|

| 5 | [Probing and Steering Evaluation Awareness of Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01786) | Nguyen et al. | 2025 | `2507.01786_probing_evaluation_awareness.pdf` | Linear probes generalize beyond length and punctuation baselines, but steering evidence is mixed. |

| 10 | [Decomposing and Measuring Evaluation Awareness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23055) | Li et al. | 2026 | `2605.23055_decomposing_eval_awareness.pdf` | Separates environment, recognition, and response propensity; introduces eight independently toggleable factors. |

| 17 | [EvalDetectBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01611) | Li et al. | 2026 | `2609.01611_evaldetectbench.pdf` | Identifies generator identity and per-model elicitation as major confounds in black-box evaluation-awareness measurement. |
